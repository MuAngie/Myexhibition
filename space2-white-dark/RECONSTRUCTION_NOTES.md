## Topology

- The black lower chamber is connected to the white hall through the rear black doorway; this is directly visible in multiple photos.
- The stair and upper landing belong to the dark exhibition sequence, but the photographs do not establish every turn between them.
- IMG_3082 is therefore shown inside zone 02 as a photographic, clickable stair connection. It leads directly to the upper zone without simulating an unverified climb.
- The interactive model therefore uses three reliable zone viewpoints and a restrained transition between them. This preserves recognizable rooms without inventing a surveyed floor plan.
