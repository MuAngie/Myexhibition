# Product

## Register

brand

## Purpose

Reconstruct the photographed exhibition as three connected spatial memories: a bright industrial white hall, a black lower chamber, and a black upper landing/corridor. The visitor should recognize the contrast between exposed concrete daylight and tightly controlled luminous darkness.

## Experience Principles

1. Treat the white hall and dark rooms as distinct atmospheres, not one room with a color change.
2. Preserve the central concrete column, the black doorway, the mezzanine edge, and the stair/railing as spatial anchors.
3. Let illuminated works provide most of the light in the dark rooms.
4. Keep the interface quiet and architectural; avoid a game HUD or decorative sci-fi effects.
5. Distinguish photographic evidence from inferred circulation and avoid claims of measured accuracy.

## Interaction Scope

Desktop-first mouse and keyboard navigation. Mouse drag looks around, wheel and WASD move, zone controls provide reliable access to every reconstructed space, and artworks support approach plus original-image viewing. Mobile touch navigation is out of scope unless requested later.
