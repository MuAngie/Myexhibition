# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The exhibition visitor is revisiting a personally meaningful show on a desktop computer. The primary job is to recover the spatial feeling of the gallery and recognize the placement of photographed works.

## Product Purpose

Reconstruct the photographed exhibition as a mouse-controlled digital space. Success means the blue gallery, dark timber floor, open ceiling, track lighting, partition walls, and artwork sequence are immediately recognizable without claiming architectural precision that the photographs cannot support.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, nocturnal, tactile. The experience should feel like returning to a real place through memory.

## Anti-references

Avoid a game HUD, generic virtual-tour chrome, sci-fi neon, decorative glass panels, or a flat photo gallery disguised as a 3D experience.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve photographic evidence before inventing detail.
2. Keep the room faithful and place the poetry in light, atmosphere, and pacing.
3. Let the artwork remain the brightest information in the scene.
4. Make navigation discoverable, then let the interface recede.
5. State uncertainty through restraint rather than false architectural detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Desktop-first mouse navigation with clear instructions, keyboard fallback, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support. Mobile touch navigation is intentionally deferred.
